🧭 The basics

The tracker is a web app. There's nothing to install — open the link and it works on a phone, tablet, or computer, and everyone sees the same live information.

  • It saves by itself. Every change syncs for the whole ward within a second. You'll see "Synced" with the time at the top.
  • Two languages. Tap 🇹🇴 Lea Faka Tonga at the top to switch between English and Tongan.
  • Light or night. The 🌙 / ☀️ button switches between the bright temple look and a darker evening look.
  • Add to your home screen. In your phone browser, choose "Add to Home Screen" and it opens like a normal app.
  • Friends vs Returning. Most tabs have a toggle between 👤 Friends (people being taught or met) and 💙 Returning (members coming back).
Good to know
Almost everything is open to everyone so the whole ward can help. Only a few things are locked with a code — adding photos, adding to the Covenant Path, and the leaders' huddle. Those codes are listed near the end of this guide.

Tab 1👥 Friends — the weekly plan

This is where the work starts. Each person has a card, and you plan their week day by day.

Set up a person's card

  • Name — type it right on the card.
  • Type — New Friend, Part Member, or OAY (over-aged youth).
  • Organization — who is responsible (Elders Quorum, Relief Society, and so on). This is what the Action Board sorts by.
  • Next Ordinance — the covenant you're helping them toward: Sacrament Meeting, Baptism & Confirmation, Aaronic Priesthood, Melchizedek Priesthood, Baptism for the Dead Recommend, Temple Endowment, or Family Sealing. Below it you'll see the doctrine that prepares them, plus a box for your own focus (a scripture, lesson, or talk).

Plan the week

  1. On a day of the week, tap ＋ Plan.
  2. Pick one or more missions from Love, Share, Invite, and Progress.
  3. Type who will make the contact (it suggests the missionaries and organizations).
  4. Add the doctrine to teach or how it will happen, then tap Add to plan.

The plan shows on that day in its lane color, and it immediately appears on the This Week tab and the Action Board.

Mark it done

  • Tap the task (here, on This Week, or on the Action Board), choose Completed or Not done, and save.
  • On Completed, pick the day it actually happened — it defaults to the planned day, so past weeks stay accurate — and set the next follow-up date.
  • The day turns green (or red for not done), and the completed act is counted in the month it was done.
Tip
Use the week arrows at the top to plan ahead or to fill in a week that already passed. Completing a past task records the real date, not today.

Prefer the old grid? The Quarterly and Full Year buttons show every person's indicators by month.

Tab 2📋 This Week

Everything planned for the current week, gathered in one place and grouped by day. You don't plan here — you work the plan.

  • Each row shows the person and their next ordinance, plus How (the activity), Who (the name and organization), When, and the doctrine to teach.
  • Tap ✅ or ✖️ on a row to mark it done or not done without leaving the page.
  • The tiles at the top show Overdue follow-ups, Planned, Tasks completed, and Awaiting date.
  • Share plan creates a clean summary you can paste into the missionary WhatsApp chat.

🗂️ Action Board

A sub-view of This Week (tap Action Board). It shows every organization's plan for the week, one box each.

  • See at a glance what the EQ, RS, Young Men, Young Women, Primary, Sunday School, and YSA each have planned.
  • An organization with nothing planned says "No plans yet this week," so gaps are easy to spot in a council meeting.
  • Tap any task to update it.

Tab 3✨ Covenant Path

For members who are baptized — recently or years ago — and the steps still ahead of them.

Add a baptized member

  1. Tap Add person at the top (this is locked — enter the code).
  2. Type the name and the real baptism date (any past date works).
  3. Pick the type (Male, Female, OAY) and the organization, then Add to Covenant Path.

The form stays open so you can add several in a row. Anyone you mark Baptized on the Friends tab also moves here automatically.

Track their progress

Each card tracks the ordinances ahead — the priesthood for the brethren, temple baptisms, patriarchal blessing, the endowment, and the sealing — plus a covenant-needs checklist (a ministering friend, a class companion, a calling, My Covenant Path), self-reliance steps, and notes.

Locked
Adding to the Covenant Path needs the code r24 so people aren't added by accident. Editing and removing existing cards is open.

Tab 4🎉 Calendar

Ward activities and events. Add an event with its date, time, and place, and it shows up in the weekly summary you share, so friends are invited to what's coming.

Tab 5✉️ Invite

Two things live here: the Church's Love · Share · Invite library, and three ward challenges.

The library

  • A video at the top. A leader can change it with the Change video button — upload a video or paste a YouTube link, and it stays for everyone (unlock with the Photos code first).
  • The articles, grouped for Everyone, Leaders, and Sharing. Each opens the official page on ChurchofJesusChrist.org, so the wording is always exact and current.

The three challenges

Each one now has a Completed button — when someone finishes, their name moves into a "Completed" list with the date, and it counts in the totals.

  • 21-Day Promise — pray morning and evening for 21 days for missionary moments. Track who's taking it, with start and finish dates.
  • Book of Mormon Hand-Out — write your testimony in a copy and give it away. Track copies given and invitations.
  • Reading Check-in — a daily reading streak, with a Readers list showing everyone's progress.
On a phone
If an article opens to only the first page, tap the ↗ to open it full screen on the Church site.

Tab 6📊 Dashboard

The numbers, updated the moment any task is marked.

  • Weekly Plan at the top: Planned, Tasks completed, Not done, Follow-through %, and People with no plan.
  • Completed-versus-planned bars by lane (Love / Share / Invite / Progress), by organization, and over the last four weeks.
  • Individual Progress — a bar for each person's activity through the year.
  • Scope toggle for Friends, Returning, or Both.

🤝 Leaders & the weekly huddle

A tap from the top opens the leadership huddle — the weekly coordination agenda built from everyone on the tracker.

  • The four coordination groups (finding, teaching, returning, new members) fill in from the Friends, Returning, and Covenant Path lists.
  • For each person: the organization, a helper, the next ordinance, the plan of action, and a date.
  • The note taker updates the plan during the huddle, marks it done, and posts the summary to the missionary chat.

Pair this with the Action Board (who's doing what this week) and the Dashboard (how it's going) and a whole coordination meeting fits on one screen.

🐢 Primary Turtle Trek

Primary has its own app, made for children. It lives at its own link and works the same way — open it, no install.

  • Tap the class, then the child's name (a parent or teacher can do it for them).
  • The child taps a mission they did — pray for a friend, make a new friend, invite a friend to Primary — in Love, Share, and Invite colors.
  • Their turtle grows from egg to captain, they earn stickers, and their class swims to the island together (no child is ranked last).
  • A Big Screen view is made for sharing time; a Leader page sets the goal, celebration, and classes.
Note
The old boat, car, and reading races are now Primary-only, as one app. Youth and adults use the challenges on the tracker's Invite tab instead.

Use the 📥 and 📤 buttons at the top to export or import the tracker's data as a file. Export once in a while so you always have a copy.

❓ Questions

Do I need an account or password?

No. Open the link and start. Only the few actions above use a code.

Will my changes show up for everyone?

Yes — everything saves live. If two people edit at the same time, both changes are kept.

A task got marked complete on the wrong day.

Tap the task, keep it on Completed, and change the "Completed on" date. It updates the calendar and the monthly totals.

Someone declined — do I delete them?

No. Mark the declined invitation and they move to "Keep loving." Keep serving; they return on their own when they choose to act.

How do I add a member who was baptized last year?

Covenant Path → Add person (code r24), then enter their real baptism date.

The video on the Invite tab won't play.

A leader taps Photos to unlock, then Change video, and uploads it or pastes a YouTube link. It then plays for everyone.

Can I print this guide?

Yes — the Print button up top makes a clean copy.
