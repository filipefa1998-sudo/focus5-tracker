Why this matters

One ward. One goal. Five for 2026.

The Savior's invitation is simple enough for a child and deep enough for a lifetime: love, share, and invite. The tracker gives the whole ward one place to do it together — so no friend is forgotten and every small act is seen.

👀

🤝

Everyone has a part

📈

Small things add up

Each visit, text, and invitation is logged and rolls into the ward's progress.

How the work flows

Plan once. It carries everywhere.

You plan on the Friends tab. The same task shows up for the week, for the whole ward, and in the numbers — automatically.

👥

1 · Plan

Tap a day, pick Love / Share / Invite, add who and the doctrine.

📋

2 · This Week

Every plan appears by day: how, who, when, what to teach.

🗂️

3 · Action Board

Each organization sees its own plans for the week.

📊

4 · Dashboard

Completed work rolls into lane, organization, and 4-week trends.

Mark a task complete from any tab — on the real day it happened — and it updates the calendar, the board, and the monthly totals at once.

Elders Quorum

The ministering engine of the rescue

Brethren carry the visits, the rides, and the priesthood path. The EQ owns the men and families who need a brother at the door.

Your move this week

Assign each less-active brother and part-member family a ministering visit, then a ride or invite to church. Log it the day it happens.

Indicators you'll live in
🚪 Visit🤝 Serve⛪ To church👔 Meet missionaries

On the Covenant Path you track each man toward the Aaronic and Melchizedek Priesthood and the temple.

🏆 The advantage: no brother is "someone else's assignment." The Action Board shows exactly who the EQ has, and who hasn't been seen.
🌸
Relief Society

Where love is felt first

Sisters notice who is hurting, who is new, and who sat alone. The RS turns ministering into belonging.

Your move this week

Welcome and sit with a new or returning sister, then follow with a meal, a text, or a comforting visit. Use "Keep loving" for anyone who said "not now."

Indicators you'll live in
👋 Welcome🫂 Comfort🙏 Pray for🏠 FHE / dinner

Part-member families are often a sister away from the next step. The tracker keeps that sister's name on the plan.

🏆 The advantage: a warm welcome stops being luck. The board makes sure every new face has a sister assigned before next Sunday.
🎯
Young Men

Friends inviting friends

The best missionary for a teenage boy is a teenage boy. The YM turn activities and quorums into open doors.

Your move this week

Each young man invites one friend to a mutual activity, then includes someone new in the group. Leaders help them log it.

Indicators you'll live in
🎉 To an activity⛪ To church👋 Welcome

On the Covenant Path the quorum watches each boy toward being ordained a deacon, teacher, and priest.

🏆 The advantage: invitations become a habit the quorum can see and celebrate — not a once-a-year push.
⭐
Young Women

Include one more

Young women change lives by making room. The YW turn class and camp into a circle a friend wants to join.

Your move this week

Invite a friend to a class or activity, and reach out to a young woman who's been away. Over-aged youth get a spot on the Covenant Path.

Indicators you'll live in
🎉 To an activity📱 Text / call💬 Gospel talk

The "needs contact" flag makes sure a young woman who's drifting is never out of sight.

🏆 The advantage: belonging is tracked like any other goal — so no young woman is quietly missed.
Primary · its own app

🐢 Primary Turtle Trek

Even the children are missionaries — in ways that fit a seven-year-old.

🙏Simple missions: pray for a friend, make a new friend, invite a friend to Primary.
🏅Every mission grows their turtle and earns a sticker.
🏝️The whole class reaches the island together — no child is ranked last.
🏆 Children learn the pattern of love, share, invite young — and a parent taps it in at home.

Sunday School

Where the doctrine lands

Teachers turn the "Next Ordinance" doctrine into a lesson a friend remembers, and make sure a member is there when the missionaries teach.

Your move this week

Use each friend's doctrine focus on the card to prepare, and sit in as a member present at a lesson.

Indicators you'll live in
💬 Gospel talk📖 Scripture📝 Taking lessons🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Member at lesson

The Invite tab holds the full Church library — the exact articles for every age group.

🏆 The advantage: lessons line up with where each person actually is on the covenant path.
💫
Young Single Adults

Peers who open the door

Young single adults reach friends no one else can. Institute, activities, and real friendship become the invitation.

Your move this week

Invite a friend to institute or a gathering, and share what your faith does for your life in a normal, natural way.

Indicators you'll live in
🎉 To an activity🏠 FHE / dinner💬 Gospel talk

The 21-Day Promise on the Invite tab is built for exactly this — pray daily for missionary moments.

🏆 The advantage: YSA efforts are counted with the whole ward's — their reach finally shows up on the board.
For every auxiliary

Come to the huddle ready in four answers

Before each weekly huddle, every auxiliary brings the same simple update for each of their friends and returning members. That is all it takes to coordinate.

🧑

1 · Who

The name — the friend or returning member you have.

🗓️

2 · When

What day this week you plan to make contact.

👤

3 · By whom

Who is responsible for making that contact.

⏭️

4 · Follow-up

When we'll check back to see if the task was completed.

What that sounds like in the huddle
Finau Mahe — plan to visit Thursday · Bro. Lutui · follow up Saturday
Mele Finau (returning) — invite to dinner Wednesday · Sis. Mafi · follow up Sunday

The tracker already holds all four — the auxiliary just reads them off their Friends tab, and the note taker checks them off.
